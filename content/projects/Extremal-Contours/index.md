Extremal Contours is an algorithmic explainable AI method for extracting compact, smooth, and interpretable visual explanations from deep vision models.

The method represents explanations as gradient-driven contours parameterized by Fourier coefficients, enabling faithful and shape-controlled attribution without model retraining.
